**Involvement - Usage**

Use `involvement` to describe the coordination record for an advisory. Define
each participating party in `entities`, and use its entity ID when identifying
the actors and recipients of actions. `entity_groups` can collect related
entities under one group ID. Record the coordination timeline in `actions`,
including the action category, date, status, and acting entities. Actions can
also refer to related products, vulnerabilities, groups, or other actions.
